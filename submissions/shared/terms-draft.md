# Proposed Epic Stocks service terms — owner review required

This draft is prepared for the operator to review, adopt and publish. It is not
an accepted agreement or a legal attestation. OpenAI requires a public terms URL;
that requirement remains blocked until the operator adopts terms and publishes
them at a stable HTTPS URL, such as https://epic-stocks.com/terms.

Suggested service description:

Epic Stocks is an independent financial recordkeeping and planning application
for Epic employees. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Epic Systems
Corporation. It does not execute securities trades, transfer money, pay loans or
file tax returns. Recording a sale or payoff changes tracking records only.

Users control their records, permissions and financial decisions. Projections
use user-entered prices, tax rates, return assumptions and other estimates;
results are uncertain and are not guarantees of future returns or personalized
professional investment, legal or tax advice. Verify records against source
statements and consult a qualified professional for decisions when needed.

Only upload information you are authorized to use. Do not submit government
identifiers, payment credentials, passwords or unrelated personal information to
AI tools. Connecting an assistant permits disclosure and changes within the
scopes selected on the consent screen. Review proposed edits before confirming.

The privacy policy describes data handling, export, disconnect and deletion.
The source code is licensed under the repository's MIT License; operating a
hosted service is distinct from distributing its source code.

Operator must supply and review: contracting identity/contact, jurisdiction,
eligibility/age rules, acceptable use, service availability, disclaimers,
liability/termination provisions, effective date and any legally required rights.
Do not paste unreviewed placeholder legal provisions into a public listing.
